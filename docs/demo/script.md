## Recording notes

- s14 and s17 are live toggles. Pre-record them and trim the wait so the mic change lands inside the scene's seconds.
- s18–s20: the real Odia exchange plays from the app. Show the Odia line as a caption: “Mamu tent wala ku baara hajaar deleni” (paid ₹12,000 to the tent vendor). Wedora replies: “₹12,000 paid to the tent vendor. Shall I record it?” Papa: “ହଁ” (yes). Wedora: “Recorded. ₹12,000 has been sent to Mamu, the Decoration head, for approval, as it is above ₹5,000.”
- Pronunciation: Wedora (weh-DOR-ah), NudgeBee (one word), Sarvam, Saaras, Bulbul, Nubi, Mamu (MAA-moo).
- The Mohanty family, Priya and Mamu are an illustrative story, not real users.
